the actual week

What a week looks like from the inside.

Not a methodology diagram. This is the order things happen in.

  1. 01

    The tape lands

    Raw recording in, usually two or more tracks because the host and guest record separately. Sync, then read the transcript end to end before touching a timeline. The read is where the clips and the on-screen cards come from, and it is also where a claim that needs checking gets caught.

    Day one
  2. 02

    The long-form gets cut

    Filler and retakes out, structure tightened, tangents removed, and back-references checked so a cut early on does not leave a callback dangling later. Audio cleaned and mixed. Graphics built for the specific things this guest explained.

    Days one to three
  3. 03

    Five verticals get assembled

    Chosen from the transcript for what actually teaches. Each one is a composite: the question stitched to the strongest answer beats from wherever they sit in the tape. Then hook graphics, covers, captions.

    Days three to four
  4. 04

    Thumbnails and the compliance pass

    Thumbnails iterated until signed off, with the topic always legible. Then everything gets checked against what this vertical's platforms actually allow, per network, before a single item is queued.

    Day four
  5. 05

    It publishes

    Titles, descriptions, tags and per-platform captions written, everything scheduled and staggered rather than dumped at once. Then the next tape lands.

    Day five
operations · delivered Live

Miraluz Studio

A photo and film studio rental in Quezon City, run by Alec Ilustre. Strong brand, no infrastructure behind it. Bookings arrived as Instagram DMs, there was no record of who had booked or paid, and there was no website at all.

Before

  • Bookings negotiated one DM at a time
  • No central record of slots or payments
  • No confirmation, so clients were left guessing
  • Receipts collected ad hoc and easily lost
  • No website, so no public rates and nothing to send a cold enquiry

After

  • A three-step booking form feeding a structured ledger
  • Live availability, so a taken slot cannot be double-booked
  • Payment page with bank transfer and QR, receipt uploaded by the client
  • Status flips to Receipt Received on its own; a styled confirmation email fires on approval
  • A five-page site on its own domain, and the owner's only action is changing one cell

Built on Google Sheets, Apps Script and Drive, so there are zero new subscriptions and nothing to migrate off. It is live at miraluzstudio.com and you can go book a slot right now. That checkability is the point of including it.

Stated plainly: this was a pro-bono build. Booking volume and hours saved are the client's numbers and not ours to publish, so we are not going to invent a percentage. The evidence here is the working system, not a retrofitted metric.
