Why it is not named here.

Because it is an employment relationship, not an agency engagement, and it is not ours to advertise. Everything else about it is describable and we will open the channel on a call. We would rather say that than build a logo wall we have not earned.

Web and systems · delivered

Miraluz Studio, and the end of booking by DM.

A daylight studio rental in Quezon City with two rooms, real demand, and a booking process that lived entirely inside one person's Instagram inbox.

Before

  • Every booking negotiated by hand in a message thread
  • Enquiries arriving after hours and waiting until morning
  • Availability held in the owner's head, so double bookings were possible
  • Payment receipts chased manually, one screenshot at a time
  • No page to send anybody, so the pitch was retyped every time

After

  • The site takes the booking and blocks the slot immediately
  • The payment receipt is collected as part of the same flow
  • The confirmation sends itself, at any hour, without anybody on shift
  • Rates, rooms and rules are on a page instead of in a thread
  • The owner's only manual action is changing one cell

Pro bono, and checkable. It is live at miraluzstudio.com. Go and book a slot, then come back and decide whether the flow is any good.
