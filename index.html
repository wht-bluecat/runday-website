under the hood

How your automations actually run.

One inquiry, end to end. Scroll to walk the same pipeline we wire up for you, from the moment a message lands to the number on your dashboard.

01 Ingest

Every inquiry hits one endpoint.

Messenger, Instagram, your web form, even walk-ins. Each lands in one place, cleaned up and saved, so nothing slips by.

02 Classify & route

AI reads the intent and sends it the right way.

It tells a hot lead from a support question from spam, scores it, and routes each one down its own branch.

03 Execute

It replies, books, and follows up in parallel.

An on-brand answer in seconds, a booking link, a reminder for later. Three actions fire at once, day and night.

04 Observe

A human keeps it honest. You see everything.

Low-confidence cases go to a person, the rest auto-resolve, and every action lands on one dashboard.
