The actual problem

Three jobs, and nobody whose job they are.

Past a certain size a business needs marketing and operations to happen every week. It rarely needs three full-time people to make that true, and it never has the budget to find out.

Symptom one

01
Everything waits for you

Nothing ships unless the owner does it, or chases the person who was supposed to. The whole plan runs at the speed of one already-full calendar.

Symptom two

02
You bought pieces, not a department

An editor here, a web person there, a virtual assistant somewhere else. Nobody owns the outcome, and you became the project manager you never wanted to be.

Symptom three

03
The busy months hide it

Marketing is the first thing dropped when work picks up, which is exactly when it should not be. Then it is quiet again and there is nothing in the pipe.

The work is not optional. The headcount is.

A United States production agency starts around $5,000 a month for the video alone, and a developer is a separate hire again. Runday is one team on one retainer, starting at $2,000 a month.

Who this is for

Is this you?

The right side of this table is not modesty. Saying yes to the wrong engagement is how a two-person studio stops being good at anything.

We are a fit if

  • The business already works, and there is a marketing or operations job nobody owns
  • You are choosing between hiring somebody and letting the work keep sliding
  • You want one team accountable for the outcome, not four freelancers to coordinate
  • You will give feedback once a week and let us run the rest of it
  • You are comparing this against a salary, not against an hourly rate

We are not a fit if

  • You want the cheapest version. There is always a cheaper version and it is not us
  • There is nothing to work with yet. Come back when there is a product and some customers
  • You need somebody to decide what the business should say. We produce, we do not run your strategy
  • You want promises about revenue or reach. We control what ships, not what an algorithm does with it
  • You need more than two engagements started this quarter. We are small on purpose
