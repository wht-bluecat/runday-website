who you are hiring

Small, and saying so.

Runday is a production studio in Metro Manila. It is small. The person who cuts your episode is the person you talk to, and there is no account manager in between.

Most agency sites in this category open with a number: four hundred companies served, three thousand shows launched. We do not have a number like that and we are not going to imply one. What we have is narrower and, for the thing you are actually buying, more useful.

Runday runs the entire weekly output for a US grower equipment brand's interview show. Not the editing. The whole department: the long-form cut, five verticals, the motion graphics, the thumbnails a client signs off on, the on-screen cards, the per-platform captions, the scheduling, and the compliance judgment that a regulated vertical demands. Eleven episodes, no missed weeks.

That is one client. It is also the most relevant possible experience for the job on this site, because it is the same job, done every week, in the same industry, under the same platform rules that will apply to yours.

The founder, James Yaptangco, is also a working content creator, which is where the read on what actually performs comes from. That matters more than it sounds: knowing which ten seconds of a fifty-minute tape will hold a scroll is not an editing skill, and it is not one an auto-clipper has.

how we work

Four things we will not do.

Stated up front, because each one is something this industry does routinely and each one would eventually cost you.

01

Claim a team we do not have

No stock staff photos, no "our team of editors", no invented headcount. If you ask how many people work on your show, the honest answer is the one you will get, and it will not change after you sign.

02

Quietly subcontract your show

The worry behind "is it just you" is usually that the work is being passed to a stranger you never approved. It is not. That is exactly why the capacity limit is two clients rather than ten.

03

Promise numbers we do not control

We will not forecast your downloads, your revenue or your subscriber growth. We control what ships, how consistently, and whether it is compliant. An algorithm decides the rest and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling.

04

Compete on being cheap

There is always someone offshore who will do it for less, and increasingly there is a tool that will do a worse version for $29 a month. Neither is what this is. We sell the pipeline and the judgment, never the clipping.
