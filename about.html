About

Small, and saying so.

Runday is a studio in Metro Manila that works United States hours and takes two clients at a time. The person who does your work is the person you talk to. There is no bench, no account layer and no second office, and pretending otherwise would fall apart on the first call anyway.

The rules

Four things we will not do.

These are not values on a wall. Each one has cost us something, which is the only reason they are worth writing down.

One

Claim a team we do not have

No stock photos of a standup, no "our team of specialists". Two clients at a time is the honest capacity of the people who are actually here, and it is on the pricing section rather than buried.

Two

Quietly subcontract your work

If something ever needs a specialist we do not have, you will be told before it happens and you can say no. What you will not get is a stranger cutting your episode because the calendar got tight.

Three

Promise numbers we do not control

We can promise what ships, when it ships and how good it is. Views, downloads, leads and revenue depend on your product, your market and an algorithm, and anybody guaranteeing those is selling you a coin flip.

Four

Compete on being cheap

There is always a cheaper option, and at the bottom of that market the work is done by somebody who will burn out or disappear. We would rather lose the deal than win it by being the lowest number in the inbox.

Practicalities

Working with us from the United States.

The distance is the thing people ask about first, so here it is answered before you have to.

Hours

Twelve to fifteen hours ahead

Work handed over at the end of your day is waiting when you start the next one. Meetings are scheduled in your morning or your evening, whichever you prefer, and we do not ask you to take a call at midnight.

Language

English, and in writing

Everything important lands as text you can search later: the notes, the decisions, the file locations. Calls are for the things that genuinely need a conversation, which is fewer than most agencies pretend.

Ownership

All of it is yours

Project files, source assets, repositories, domains, hosting and logins are created in your name from day one. Ending the engagement is a permissions change, not a negotiation, and there is no exit invoice.

Under the hood

What the work is built with.

Named because you should be able to check, and because a tool list is a fair proxy for whether somebody has actually done the job.

Video

Cut, mixed and animated

DaVinci Resolve and Descript for the edit and the transcript-driven work. Remotion for motion graphics, which means the animations are code and can be regenerated rather than rebuilt by hand every week.

Web

Fast by default

Plain HTML and CSS where a framework would only add weight, and a real framework where the app needs one. Hosted on a global edge network, in your account, with the source in a repository you own.

Systems

Automation, watched

Workflow automation and scripted pipelines wired into the tools you already use, plus AI where it genuinely saves time and nowhere it would quietly get something wrong. A person checks the output.

The unglamorous half of this is repeatable tooling: the same checks, the same file structure, the same publishing routine every week. It is the only reason a two-person studio can hold a weekly cadence at all.

The offer

Judge it on the work, not on this page.

Send something you have already published and get one finished piece back, free. It is a faster way to find out whether this is any good than reading another about page.
