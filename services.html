the delivery list

Everything that ships, every week.

This is the whole scope, written the way it is actually delivered rather than the way it would sound biggest. If something is not on this page, it is not included, and we will say so on the call rather than after you have signed.

01
the long-form

The episode itself

You upload the raw recording and stop thinking about it. What comes back is a finished cut, not a rough assembly for you to fix.

cut

The edit

  • Filler words, false starts and retakes removed
  • Structural edits: reordering, tightening, cutting the tangent that goes nowhere
  • Back-references checked, so a cut earlier in the tape does not orphan a callback later
  • Multi-track sync when a remote guest records on their own device
sound

The audio

  • Cleaned, levelled and mixed across every speaker
  • Room noise, plosives and handling noise dealt with
  • Intro and outro in place, music bedded under
  • An audio-only master, if you publish to podcast platforms too
02
five per episode

The verticals

The important part: these are composite cuts. An auto-clipper can only take one continuous slice of the tape, which is why its clips so often start mid-thought and end before the point lands. We stitch the host's question to the strongest answer beats from anywhere in the episode, around one idea.

cut

How they are built

  • Chosen from the transcript for what teaches, not what is loudest
  • Question plus answer, assembled from separate parts of the tape
  • Reframed to 1080 by 1920 with the speaker actually in frame
  • Burned-in captions, styled to the channel
art

What comes with each

  • A cold-open hook graphic, keyed to transparent
  • A cover image in the channel's own caption style
  • B-roll placement, with a search list when stock is needed
  • A per-platform caption set, not one caption pasted four times
03
motion and stills

The graphics

Built per episode for the thing being explained, not pulled from a template pack. When a guest describes a mechanism that has no footage, we animate the mechanism.

motion

Animated explainers

Full-screen cutaways for the abstract ideas, and transparent overlays that sit on top of the speaker. Rendered with alpha so they drop straight onto a timeline.

motion

Lower thirds and callouts

Names and titles, product callouts with the real product photo, end cards and subscribe prompts. Consistent across every episode.

stills

Thumbnails

Iterated until you sign them off. The topic is always legible, because a punch line on its own does not tell anyone what the video is about.

stills

On-screen text cards

A header and two to four pointer bullets per topic, timed to the transcript, so a viewer watching muted still follows the argument.

04
out the door

Publishing

The part most editors hand back to you. Views are impressions multiplied by click-through, and both of those are decided after the edit is finished.

copy

Written per platform

  • Titles written against the channel's own view data, not taste
  • Descriptions with chapters that match the cut
  • Tags and hashtags per network
  • Separate caption sets for YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and X
time

Scheduled

  • Queued in your timezone, at the slot that performs on your channel
  • Long-form and verticals staggered rather than dumped at once
  • A compliance pass before anything goes live
  • You keep final approval on every item
